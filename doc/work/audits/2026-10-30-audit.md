  allocation suite exercises exact threshold and remainder boundaries, tie

- The full existing `npm test` suite passed: 39 files, 2,465 tests. It does not

  establish that typed party shares survive the questionnaire build.
- Reproduced by calling the actual `deriveElectorate` and `runSeats` in the
  current TypeScript modules. Two parties have input shares 3.25% and 96.75%,
  a 3.25% threshold, two groups at 70% turnout, and party compositions
  1% / 99% and 5% / 95%. The rounded group inputs yield the first party's
  exact share `81249731/2500000000` (3.24998924%), below the threshold.
  The engine assigns seats 0 / 120; at the entered shares Hamilton assigns
  4 / 116. The voting and allocation modules operate exactly on their received
  inputs, but the questionnaire conversion loses the authoritative share.
